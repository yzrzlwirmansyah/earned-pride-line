One quiet line of earned pride: after two years of physio, Dani takes his first unaided steps across his own kitchen while his wife holds her breath at the door. No trophy, no podium, no congratulations — just a long effort finally landing, in a single sentence that lets the pride arrive by the last word.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line plus a trailing newline to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), then re-reads the file and verifies it matches exactly.
